# University AI Policy

The university permits students to use AI assistants for brainstorming, outlining, and grammar review when the instructor allows it. Students must disclose material AI assistance in submitted work.

AI-generated text may not be submitted as original analysis without verification, citation, and instructor approval. Course teams may require students to provide prompts, intermediate drafts, or tool logs during academic integrity reviews.

Research groups handling sensitive data must follow the university data privacy standard. Personally identifiable information, unpublished research data, and confidential meeting notes must not be pasted into public AI systems unless an approved enterprise agreement is in place.

Faculty should evaluate AI-supported assignments using transparent rubrics that reward reasoning, evidence quality, and source attribution.
